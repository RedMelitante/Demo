About us

A gardening business built on sustainability

Organic Gardening Australia started in 2018 with a simple idea: if we're going to look after gardens, we should do it in a way that's good for the people living there and for the soil underneath.

That means no synthetic weed killers or pesticides, plenty of compost, and plants chosen for Melbourne's conditions. We also show you easy ways to keep your garden healthy between our visits, so it relies less on any kind of treatment.

FAQ

Questions we often get

What does "chemical-free" gardening mean? +

We don't use synthetic weed killers, pesticides or fertilisers. Weeds are removed by hand or smothered with mulch, soil is fed with compost and organic matter, and pests are managed with good plant choices and natural methods.

Is it safe for my kids and pets straight after a visit? +

Yes. Because we skip the sprays, there's no waiting period before the kids, dogs or chooks head back outside.

How much does it cost? +

It depends on the size of your garden, how often you'd like us and what needs doing. Send us a few photos through the form or WhatsApp and we'll give you a clear quote before any work starts.

Do you do one-off jobs or only regular maintenance? +

Both. Many clients start with a one-off clean-up, then keep us on weekly, fortnightly or monthly to keep things in shape.

Do you take away green waste? +

Yes. Where we can, green waste is composted or recycled rather than sent to landfill. We can also set up a compost system so your garden waste feeds your own soil.

When is the best time to start a garden in Melbourne? +

Autumn and spring are ideal for planting while the soil is warm and rain is more reliable. Summer is the time to mulch and protect, and winter is great for soil prep, pruning and planting bare-rooted fruit trees.

FAQ

Common questions

Are your quotes really free?+

Yes. We visit your property, talk through what you want and send a written quote with no obligation.

How much does landscaping cost in Melbourne?+

It depends on size, access, levels and materials. A garden clean-up is a very different job to a full backyard build with paving and walls, so we price every project after seeing the site. Share a rough budget in the form and we’ll suggest what’s achievable.

When is the best time to landscape?+

Hard landscaping like paving, walls and decks can be done most of the year. Autumn and spring are ideal for planting and new turf in Melbourne, as plants settle in before the summer heat. Spring tends to book up quickly.

Can you fix a yard that stays wet and boggy?+

Often, yes. Heavy clay soils are common across Melbourne and drain slowly. We look at levels and where water is going, then suggest options such as regrading, drainage lines or raised beds.

Do I need a permit for a retaining wall or deck?+

Some walls and decks need a building permit depending on height, size and location. We’ll flag this during the quote and point you in the right direction with your council.

Are you insured?+

Yes, we carry public liability insurance. Ask us for a copy of the certificate with your quote.

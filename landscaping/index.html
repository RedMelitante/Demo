Soil first, always.

Every landscaping job starts with the ground, so new plants settle in and stay healthy.

What we build

Landscaping that works with Melbourne's conditions

Heavy clay, sandy bayside soil, hot northerlies and frosty mornings: we choose plants and materials that cope with all of it.

Garden design & planting

A planting plan matched to your sun, soil and space, then built and planted by our team.

Native & water-wise gardens

Hardy natives and drought-tolerant plants that get through a Melbourne summer and bring in birds and bees.

Raised veggie beds & kitchen gardens

Timber raised beds filled with rich organic soil, ready for herbs, veggies and little hands.

Paths, edging & garden beds

Neat edges, mulched beds and simple paths that tie the garden together and cut down on weeding.

Soil preparation & renovation

Compost, organic matter and gypsum for clay: we fix tired or compacted ground before anything goes in.

Garden makeovers

Overgrown or tired garden? We clear, rebuild and replant, then keep it looking good with regular care.

How it works

How a landscaping project runs

  1. 01

    Chat & photos

    Tell us what you'd like and send a few photos of the space.

  2. 02

    Site visit & plan

    We check soil, sun and drainage, then put together a plan and written quote.

  3. 03

    Build & plant

    We prepare the ground, build and plant, chemical-free, and clean up as we go.

  4. 04

    Settle in

    You get care tips for the first few months, or we can look after it for you.
